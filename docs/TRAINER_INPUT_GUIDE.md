## Step 2: Send Real CV Samples (Optional But Valuable)

**Why we need them:**
Your region is different. A "good CV" in Berlin might be bad in Vienna. We use your real examples to:
1. Show participants GOOD examples (so they learn what to aim for)
2. Show participants BAD examples (so they don't make common mistakes)
3. Customize quick-fill buttons ("Did you work with X?")

**What to send:**
- 3-5 CVs per participant type (unemployed, career switcher, student, etc.)
- **Anonymized** (remove name, contact, anything identifying)
- Can be documents, screenshots, or text
- One email to: [contact to be filled in]

**Subject**: "AMS JobAssist — CV Samples for [Your AMS Name]"

## How We Use This

### Your 8 Answers → We Build

1. **Interview questions** — Customized for your participant types
2. **Good/bad examples** — From your region's real CVs (if provided)
3. **Quick-fill buttons** — Based on your skills + jobs
4. **AMS rules** — Built into the tool automatically
5. **Language handling** — Set for German + your participants' languages
6. **Teaching tool** — Trainer comparison view for your review process

### Timeline

- **Day 1**: You send us this form + (optional) CV samples
- **Days 2-5**: We customize the tool for your AMS
- **Week 2**: You test with 1-2 participants
- **Week 3**: Small feedback → we refine
- **Week 4**: Ready to launch with your full cohort

---

## Why This Matters

**The tool is ONLY as good as the examples and decisions we build it with.**

Generic examples = participants feel it's not for them  
Generic questions = participants don't see themselves in it  
No AMS rules built in = trainer has to fix everything manually

**Your real CVs + your real rules = tool that actually works for your participants.**

---

## What NOT To Do

❌ Don't overthink this. First instinct is usually right.  
❌ Don't be vague. "Better examples" is vague. "CVs from warehouse workers and office staff" is clear.  
❌ Don't worry about perfection. We iterate. Send what you have.  
❌ Don't wait for perfect samples. 2-3 examples per type is enough to start.
